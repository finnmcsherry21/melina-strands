Puzzles

    How to play

    Find the hidden words that fit the theme. Drag (or tap letter by letter, then tap the last letter again) to connect letters — up, down, sideways or diagonally.

    Blue = theme word. Yellow = the spangram, which describes the theme and touches two opposite sides of the board.

    Every letter is used exactly once. Stuck? Every 3 wrong guesses of 4+ letters earns a hint ♥
